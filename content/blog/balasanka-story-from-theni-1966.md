Most of our customers know us by a showroom name: a TVS or Honda dealership, Akshaya Velli, Tanishq or Titan. Fewer know that all of them grew from one small tamarind business in Theni, started nearly sixty years ago.

## 1966: SGT, Theni

In 1966, Mr. S. Thirupathi Nadar started SGT, a small tamarind business in Theni. It was a trade where customers judged a seller by the weight on the scale and the grade of the produce. Honest weight and fair grading became the family's way of doing business, and they still are.

## From tamarind to pulses

In 1991 the family started Balasanka Dhal, processing pulses, and set up what was then Asia's first solar dryer for pulses. Sanka Dhal followed in 1995 and Akshaya Dhal in 2016. In 2001, Sri Balasanka Mills began turning tamarind seeds, which used to be thrown away, into starch for textile printing and husk for fuel.

## Two-wheelers

Our first two-wheeler dealership, Shri Murugesan Motors, opened in Theni in 1997. Between 2004 and 2017, Balasanka TVS, Mukesh Motors (Honda), Yasotha Motors and the Tenkasi TVS dealership took our two-wheeler business to six towns. Today it is the largest part of the group, with sales, service and genuine spares in every showroom.

## Jewellery, watches and eyewear

In 2019 our first jewellery store, Tanishq, opened in Theni. Titan, Titan Eye+, Mia by Tanishq and CaratLane stores followed. In 2021 we opened Akshaya Velli in Theni, our own silver jewellery house, and then took it to Tenkasi, Tirunelveli and Sivakasi. In 2025, Zora by Akshaya Velli opened in Theni for silver gifts and pooja articles.

## Today

The group now runs 23 showrooms in Madurai, Rajapalayam, Sivakasi, Srivilliputhur, Tenkasi, Theni, Tirunelveli and Virudhunagar. The businesses are very different, but the idea from 1966 hasn't changed: what we sell should be exactly what the label says.
